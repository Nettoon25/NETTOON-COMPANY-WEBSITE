The idea behind Nettoon

Not another everything-platform.
A home for animation.

Animation is a medium, an art form, a culture, and a way to imagine what does not exist yet. It deserves a destination designed around its full range.

Nettoon is being built around one clear focus: cartoons and animation. From short-form experiments to episodic worlds and ambitious independent productions, we want to make animated storytelling easier to find and easier to share.

The creator opportunity

More than a place to upload.
A place to grow.

Great animation comes from studios, independent artists, emerging teams, and people making something extraordinary with limited resources.

Nettoon’s ambition is to help creators reach an audience that comes specifically for animation. We want to build the foundations for publishing, audience engagement, and sustainable creator opportunity over time.

  • 01A platform dedicated to animated work
  • 02Space for individual videos, shorts, and series
  • 03A long-term focus on creator tools and monetisation

Our roots. No borders.

Born in Africa.
Open to the world.

Africa is not a footnote to the future of storytelling. It is part of the future being written now.

Nettoon begins in Nairobi, Kenya, with a global outlook. Our African roots shape our perspective: many languages, many traditions, many ways of seeing the world — and an abundance of stories still waiting for their audience.

We’re not building a platform that limits stories to where they began. We’re building one that helps them travel.
