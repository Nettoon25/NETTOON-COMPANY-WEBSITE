The company

NETTOON : Your Online Domain for Free Cartoons and Animations only.

NETTOON Technologies is a technology and digital entertainment venture Company focused on building a dedicated global platform for cartoons and animation. We believe animation is more than entertainment. It is a medium for storytelling, cultural expression, education, imagination, and economic opportunity.

Our goal is to connect independent animators, established studios, emerging storytellers, and animation enthusiasts within a specialized digital ecosystem where animated content can be published, discovered, shared, and developed into lasting creative value. By bringing animation into a focused digital environment, NETTOON aims to expand access to audiences, encourage creative experimentation, and open new pathways for creators to build recognizable identities, communities, and sustainable businesses.

Our ambition begins in Nairobi, Kenya, and looks toward a global community of animation creators and audiences. We are building a home for animation — and an ecosystem for the people who bring it to life.

The Vision

Strategic Vision in a Nutshell

The global animation market is projected to reach approximately $950 billion by 2030, driven by soaring demand from streaming and gaming. While this market is booming, there is a distinct and underserved gap for independent cartoon animators lacking a dedicated, global distribution pipeline outside of major studio gatekeepers and noisy general video platforms. Nettoon's vision is to democratize animation creation and consumption globally. We will empower a new generation of content creators, particularly those from Africa and emerging markets, by providing professional-grade distribution tools and an engaged, niche-specific audience, thereby becoming the definitive launchpad for the next era of global animation talent.

The Product

Platform Blueprint.
(Product + Features)

Nettoon is designed as a cross-platform digital streaming platform accessible via mobile apps and web browsers. Its user-friendly interface offers seamless navigation, optimized video streaming, and interactive features tailored specifically for animation content consumers and creators.

The platform is engineered as a mobile-first, content-hierarchical platform that combines the viral velocity of short-form video with the revenue potential and narrative depth of premium episodic content. The user experience is designed for speed, low-bandwidth resilience, and maximum creator-viewer engagement.

Nettoon is a dual-channel streaming platform: a responsive web application (desktop + mobile browsers) and first-class native mobile apps (iOS & Android).Primary focus: cartoons & animation content in three formats (series, one-time films, short “Nettoon Shots”).

For Creators

More than a place to upload.
A place to grow.

NETTOON's creator economy is designed to ensure that creators do not simply provide content to the platform. Creators should have clear opportunities to build audiences, generate income, grow their creative businesses, and access commercial opportunities through NETTOON.

Foundations

Ownership & Localization Strategy

The Africa-first strategy ensures that while Nettoon operates globally, its creative, financial, and technological control remains firmly grounded on the continent.This model empowers local creators, strengthens our digital independence, and ensures that Africa not only consumes global culture, but creates it.
