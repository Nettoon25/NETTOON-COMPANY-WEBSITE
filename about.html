The company

NETTOON : Your Online Domain for Free Cartoons and Animations only.

NETTOON Technologies is a technology and digital entertainment venture Company focused on building a dedicated global platform for cartoons and animation. We believe animation is more than entertainment. It is a medium for storytelling, cultural expression, education, imagination, and economic opportunity.

Our goal is to connect independent animators, established studios, emerging storytellers, and animation enthusiasts within a specialized digital ecosystem where animated content can be published, discovered, shared, and developed into lasting creative value. By bringing animation into a focused digital environment, NETTOON aims to expand access to audiences, encourage creative experimentation, and open new pathways for creators to build recognizable identities, communities, and sustainable businesses.

Our ambition begins in Nairobi, Kenya, and looks toward a global community of animation creators and audiences. We are building a home for animation — and an ecosystem for the people who bring it to life.

The platform

NETTOON : Your Online Domain for Free Cartoons and Animations only.

Nettoon is a proposed global video streaming application software designed as the definitive, dedicated platform for user-generated and professionally produced Animated and Cartoon content.

Positioning itself as the "YouTube of Cartoons and Animations," Nettoon offers creators a world-class space to upload, share, and monetize content across all genres, while providing global audiences with a limitless, interactive, and personalized viewing experience.

The platform's core differentiator is its sophisticated architecture for managing long-form (Series), short-form (Shorts), and Standalone releases, each with deep, real-time user engagement metrics, fostering a truly unique digital entertainment ecosystem.

Unlike traditional streaming platforms, Nettoon focuses purely on animated content, creating a vibrant ecosystem where users can upload, watch, and download cartoons and animated films; for free.It merges creativity, technology, and community into one powerful platform designed to empower animation culture globally.

Nettoon has the potential to redefine entertainment beyond just cartoons and animations. If creators start using the platform for animated music videos, news segments, avatars for storytelling, and personal branding, it could create an entirely new media category.

Think about it, YouTube revolutionized video content, TikTok changed short-form media, and Nettoon will be the first platform to mainstream animation as a universal form of expression.
