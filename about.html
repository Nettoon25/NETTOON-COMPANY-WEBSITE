Who we are

NETTOON : Your Online Domain for Free Cartoons and Animations only.

Nettoon is a proposed global video streaming application software designed as the definitive, dedicated platform for user-generated and professionally produced Animated and Cartoon content.

Positioning itself as the "YouTube of Cartoons and Animations," Nettoon offers creators a world-class space to upload, share, and monetize content across all genres, while providing global audiences with a limitless, interactive, and personalized viewing experience. content.

The platform's core differentiator is its sophisticated architecture for managing long-form (Series), short-form (Shorts), and one-time videos, each with deep, real-time user engagement metrics, fostering a truly unique digital entertainment ecosystem. Unlike traditional streaming platforms, Nettoon focuses purely on animated content, creating a vibrant ecosystem where users can upload, watch, and download cartoons and animated films; for free.It merges creativity, technology, and community into one powerful platform designed to empower animation culture globally. content.

Nettoon has the potential to redefine entertainment beyond just cartoons and animations. If creators start using the platform for animated music videos, news segments, avatars for storytelling, and personal branding, it could create an entirely new media category. content.

Think about it, YouTube revolutionized video content, TikTok changed short-form media, and Nettoon will be the first platform to mainstream animation as a universal form of expression.

The company

One clear focus.
Many possible worlds.

We believe animation deserves more than being one category among many. It deserves a place designed around its formats, creators, cultures and communities.

Nettoon is being developed as a dedicated destination for one-time animated videos, shorts, and episodic series. Our goal is to make animated work easier to discover and follow, while building an environment where creators can grow an audience and, over time, access tools and monetisation opportunities.

We are at an early stage. Our product, team, and roadmap will develop through research, careful execution, and feedback from creators and viewers. We will share milestones and capabilities as they become confirmed.
