# NETTOON — Company Website

**Building the future of animation, starting from Africa.**

NETTOON is a technology brand focused on building digital experiences that empower animation creators, connect audiences with animated content, and help animation reach a global audience.

This repository contains the source code for the official NETTOON company website.

## About NETTOON

NETTOON is building an animation-focused digital ecosystem designed to help creators share their work and help audiences discover cartoons and animation in one dedicated destination.

Our vision is to contribute to a future where animation from Africa and around the world can reach wider audiences, build communities, and unlock new opportunities.

## Our Mission

To build digital platforms that make animation more accessible, discoverable, and rewarding for creators and audiences.

## Our Vision

To build a globally recognised animation ecosystem that starts in Africa and connects animation creators and viewers worldwide.

The NETTOON company website serves as the official online presence of the brand. It introduces the company, communicates its mission and vision, presents its strategic direction, and provides a point of contact for potential collaborators, partners, creators, and other stakeholders.
