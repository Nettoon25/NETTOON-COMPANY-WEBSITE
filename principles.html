The standards we want to earn

Ambition is a direction.

01 / FOCUS

Stay animation-first.

Make product decisions around the needs of animated storytelling, not every trend competing for attention.

02 / CREATORS

Respect the people who make it.

Build clear publishing experiences and work toward fair, transparent creator opportunities.

03 / DISCOVERY

Help great work get found.

Design discovery that gives audiences new paths into stories and creators room to be seen.

04 / RESPONSIBILITY

Build for long-term trust.

Take safety, privacy, accessibility, content rights, and platform quality seriously as the product grows.
