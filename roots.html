NETTOON / IN FOCUS

Ownership & Localization Strategy

Nettoon is a proud subsidiary of Afrikespace, rooted firmly in Africa and committed to an Africa-first localization strategy. The ownership structure ensures African control and decision-making, and reflects the region's unique cultural narratives and entertainment needs. Localizing content, platform management, and user experience for African languages and contexts is fundamental to Nettoon’s strategy, ensuring relevance and resonance with continental and global African diaspora audiences. Our strategy is to build a truly global platform by embedding deep, local relevance from day one. Our approach is not just to target African consumers, but to build a powerful engine in Africa that generates content, talent, and solutions for the world. Nettoon begins in Nairobi with an African perspective and a global outlook. The ambition is to make room for stories from many places, languages, and creative traditions.

Our roots. No borders.

The Global Roadmap :
From Vision to Global Movement.

Africa is not a footnote to the future of storytelling. It is part of the future being written now.

Nettoon begins in Nairobi, Kenya, with a global outlook. Our African roots shape our perspective: many languages, many traditions, many ways of seeing the world — and an abundance of stories still waiting for their audience.

We’re not building a platform that limits stories to where they began. We’re building one that helps them travel.
