WHY THE KNOWLEDGE HUB

A place to understand the craft behind the cartoons.

Animation is a creative discipline, a technical process and a global industry.

The NETTOON Knowledge Hub brings useful information together for aspiring animators, experienced creators, studios, students and animation enthusiasts. Its purpose is to make animation knowledge easier to discover, understand and put into practice.

Explore the foundations, learn about the tools, follow the production process, discover African animation, develop your creative skills and understand the business behind the work.
