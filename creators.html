What is an "Ecosystem" in the Context of Cartoon - Animation?

In biology, an ecosystem is a community of living organisms (biotic factors) interacting with their non-living environment (abiotic factors). Transposing this to animation, an "ecosystem" is a self-sustaining and interconnected network of:

  • 01Infrastructure/Platform (Abiotic Environment)
  • 02Creators (Producers/Biotic) - The Animators
  • 03Pillar 3: The Community (Culture Layer)
